# Design

## References (craft, not identity)

- Hicks Thomas LLP — team + expertise, serious litigation tone
- Miller Shah LLP — bold identity, photography, large type
- AttorneyX — anti-cliché (no gavels, no stock handshakes)
- Trust Guss — large type + portrait presence only; not injury-mill energy

## Signature interaction

Practice index: oversized serif names. Hover/focus swaps a large still + one-line descriptor. Mobile: stacked list with a still per item. Not an icon-card grid.

## Supporting motion

- Header shrink on scroll
- Hero line reveal
- Gold-rule draw
- Page fade via Vue transitions
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no transforms beyond opacity
- No cursor blobs, no ken-burns on every image, no 3D gavels

## Photography

- 2–3 architectural stills (Tulsa/Oklahoma, Unsplash, stored in repo)
- Attorney faces from public kanialaw.com pages when available
- Fallback: monogram portraits — never stock smiling lawyers

## Mark

Gold square K, hairline frame, wordmark, small-caps *In Your Corner*.

## Pitch chrome

Persistent banner: *Concept redesign for Kania Law Office — not the live website.*  
Sticky mobile consult bar with click-to-call.
