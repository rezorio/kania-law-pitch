# Brand

## Positioning

Oklahoma trial lawyers. Large-firm reach, small-firm attention. **In your corner.**

## Keep from the live site

- Forest green + gold/tan
- Gold square **K** mark
- Wordmark: KANIA LAW OFFICE
- Tagline: In Your Corner (editorial, no boxing-glove clipart)
- One-stop Oklahoma practice
- 24/7 availability, free consultation
- Tulsa HQ + regional offices
- Attorney roster and Ask a Lawyer

## Drop

- Clipart glove, gavels, scales-of-justice stock
- Green form brick, SEO link farms, unreadable homepage length

## Voice

Short, specific, Oklahoma. No invented verdicts. Proof only from published firm claims:

- Represented thousands of injured Oklahomans
- Recovered millions of dollars for clients
- Available 24/7
- Free phone consultation
- Immigration: English and Spanish (one line, not a second site)
